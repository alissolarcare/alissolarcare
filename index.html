Frequently Asked Questions

Why is professional solar panel cleaning important?

Dust, bird droppings, and urban smog accumulate on panels over time, blocking direct sunlight. Professional cleaning removes this residue, boosting your solar generation efficiency by up to 25%.

What is the recommended cleaning frequency?

In Karachi, due to high dust levels and environmental pollution, we recommend cleaning your solar panels at least once a month. Heavy dust areas or industrial zones benefit greatly from our bi-weekly (twice a month) package.

What cleaning methods and water are used?

We use specialized soft-brush equipment and fresh water rinses tailored explicitly for solar glass. No harsh abrasive chemicals are used, ensuring zero damage to panel seals or glass coatings.

Are panels safe during the cleaning process?

Yes, absolutely. Our technicians are trained in safe roof access and handle residential and commercial arrays with extreme care, ensuring complete equipment safety.

How is pricing calculated?

All package pricing and individual quotes are based strictly on the total number of solar panels installed on your property.

Do you provide before-and-after videos?

Yes! For transparency and customer satisfaction, we share before-and-after cleaning videos with you upon completion of the service.

Is roof cleaning or roof water cleaning included?

Roof Water Cleaning is NOT included in any standard cleaning package. It is available upon request for an additional charge.

What areas in Karachi do you service?

We provide residential and commercial solar panel cleaning services across all major areas in Karachi, Sindh, Pakistan.

How do I book or contact you?

You can easily book through our online Booking form, email us at alissolarcare@gmail.com, or chat directly with us via our floating WhatsApp button (+92 322 8132237).
